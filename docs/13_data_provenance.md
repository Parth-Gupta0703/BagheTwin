## 1. Verified Public Domain Facts vs. Synthetic Assumptions
To maintain absolute scientific integrity, BagheTwin categorizes every piece of data into three distinct tiers:

### Tier 1: Verified Public Field Context (OIL India Limited)
* Field: Baghewala Field, Bikaner-Nagaur Basin, Rajasthan.
* Formation: Jodhpur Sandstone.
* Fluid: Heavy crude oil, 17–19° API.
* Viscosity: Approximately 10,000–13,000 cP at 50°C.
* Artificial Lift & Thermal EOR: Sucker Rod Pumps coupled with Cyclic Steam Stimulation.

### Tier 2: Engineering Formulations & Literature References
* ASTM D341 / Andrade liquid viscosity relationship.
* IAPWS-IF97 steam saturation enthalpy polynomials.
* API TR 11L sucker-rod pumping kinematic standards (adapted for heavy-oil viscous annular shear).

### Tier 3: Synthetic / Configurable Demonstration Values
* Well codes (BGW-001 through BGW-012).
* Specific daily flow rates, dynamic fluid levels, and stroke loads.
* Historical telemetry and sensor timestamps.
* Economic price assumptions (₹6,200/bbl crude, ₹2,400/t steam, ₹7.5/kWh electricity).

**Strict Policy:** No synthetic metric is ever presented as real field data.
